ADD    7 BUY  10015 900

MODIFY 7 10015 150
CANCEL 4
# Market sell takes 150 @ 10015 from order 7 and 100 @ 9990 from order 6
MARKET 8 SELL 250
# IOC sell at 9985 fills against nothing (best bid is 9980) and is discarded

# FOK buy of 400 at 10020: only 300 available at that price -> killed
FOK   10 BUY  10020 400
